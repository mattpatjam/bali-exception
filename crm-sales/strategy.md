# CRM Sales — Go-to-Market Strategy

## The Opportunity

Bali Exception built a CRM for internal sales operations. It works. Now the company is monetizing it by selling subscriptions to other real estate agencies in Indonesia — starting with Jakarta and expanding outward. Matthieu earns recurring commission on each deal closed.

This is a founder-led sales motion. There's no marketing team, no ads budget, no SDR. Everything runs through:
1. Matthieu's personal brand on LinkedIn
2. Direct outbound (cold email + LinkedIn DMs)
3. Referrals from early clients

---

## Phase 1: Indonesia — Jakarta First

**Timeline:** Start immediately
**Target cities:** Jakarta (primary), Surabaya, Bandung, Medan (secondary)
**Excluded geography:** Bali, Lombok, Sumbawa, Gili Islands, and surrounding areas (competitive protection)

**Why Jakarta first:**
- Largest real estate market in Indonesia by volume
- High concentration of mid-size agencies (5–50 agents) — the sweet spot
- LinkedIn penetration is strong among Jakarta business owners
- English + Bahasa mixed communication is workable
- Distance from Bali Exception's core market = no conflict

## Sales Motion

### Channel 1: LinkedIn (Warm)

Matthieu already has a LinkedIn presence and posts 2x/week. The strategy is to:
- Add a CRM-focused content pillar (1 post/month) framed around personal experience at Bali Exception
- Run a systematic connection campaign targeting Jakarta real estate decision-makers (10–20/week)
- Use DMs to open conversations with warm leads who engage with content

This channel builds trust over weeks. It's not the fastest path to a first sale but it's the most scalable.

### Channel 2: Cold Email (Fast)

The fastest path to booked demos. Outreach directly to CEOs, owners, heads of sales, and GMs at real estate agencies.

Sequence: 3 emails over 9 days. Pain-first, social proof in email 2, soft close in email 3.

Target: 20–30 new leads/week, 3–5% reply rate = 1–2 conversations/week minimum.

### Channel 3: Referrals (TBD)

Once the first client is live, build a simple referral incentive (1 month free, fee reduction, or cash per intro). Not to build yet — lock in first client first.

## Sales Flow

```
Lead identified
    → Connection request (LinkedIn) or cold email sent
        → Reply / engagement
            → Discovery call booked (30 min)
                → Demo sent or live demo (30 min)
                    → Proposal / pricing sent
                        → Follow-up (CRM reminder)
                            → Close
```

Every active prospect goes into the Bali Exception CRM. No exceptions.

## Messaging Framework

**Core positioning:** "The CRM built by real estate agents, for real estate agents — tested daily in one of Indonesia's most competitive markets."

**Pain we solve:**
- Losing leads because follow-up happened too late (or not at all)
- Client history scattered across WhatsApp, email, and notebooks
- No visibility into which agent is working which lead
- No way to see pipeline health at a glance

**Proof point:** We use it ourselves at Bali Exception. It didn't come from a startup — it came from the field.

**Differentiators vs. HubSpot/Pipedrive:**
- Built for Indonesian real estate, not generic B2B
- No 6-month onboarding or enterprise pricing
- WhatsApp-native (not just email)
- Local support

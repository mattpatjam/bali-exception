# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Who We're Selling To

### Primary ICP — Jakarta Mid-Size Real Estate Agency

**Company profile:**
- Real estate agency based in Jakarta (not a developer — an agency)
- 5 to 50 active agents
- Handles residential sales, leasing, or both
- Lists properties on Rumah.com, Lamudi.co.id, or 99.co/id
- Has been operating for at least 2 years (established, not a startup)
- Revenue generated through commissions — not salaried sales

**Why this segment:**
- Big enough to feel the pain of disorganized lead management
- Small enough that they can't afford a $500/month HubSpot or a 3-month onboarding process
- The owner or GM is still hands-on — reachable, decision-maker, can move fast
- WhatsApp is already central to their sales process (fits our tool)

---

## Decision-Makers to Target

Priority order:

1. **CEO / Owner / Founder** — Final budget decision, fastest close if convinced
2. **Head of Sales / Sales Director** — Daily pain of managing agents; strong internal advocate
3. **General Manager (GM)** — Operational owner; often the one implementing tools

Avoid: IT managers (no budget authority, slow), junior agents (no influence)

---

## Pain Signals (What to Look For)

A prospect is hot if they show any of these:
- Agency has 5+ agents but no visible CRM or tech stack mention on LinkedIn/website
- Job postings for "sales coordinator" or "admin" to manage leads (manual process)
- Low response rate or poor follow-up reputation (mentioned in reviews or anecdotally)
- Owner is posting on LinkedIn about scaling or hiring — growth pain
- Active on property portals with high listing volume but no clear pipeline system

---

## Disqualifiers

Do NOT pursue:
- Any agency in Bali, Lombok, Sumbawa, Gili Islands, or nearby islands (competitive protection)
- Large enterprises with an existing CRM contract (HubSpot, Salesforce, custom build)
- Developers or construction companies — different sales process, not the right fit
- Agencies with fewer than 3 active agents (too small, won't pay)
- Agencies outside Indonesia (hold for Phase 2/3)

---

## Secondary ICP — Surabaya / Bandung / Medan

Same profile as Jakarta ICP. Lower LinkedIn penetration, so cold email is more important. Prioritize after 3+ Jakarta clients are live and can serve as social proof.

---

## ICP Scoring (Quick Reference)

Score each lead 1–3 on each factor:

| Factor | 1 (weak) | 2 (ok) | 3 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team size | 1–3 agents | 4–10 agents | 11–50 agents |
| City | Secondary city | Surabaya/Bandung | Jakarta |
| Decision-maker access | Unknown | Head of Sales | CEO/Owner |
| Tech maturity | Enterprise CRM | Mixed tools | Excel/WhatsApp only |
| Growth signal | Static | Hiring | Actively scaling |

**Score 10+:** Priority outreach
**Score 7–9:** Active pipeline
**Score below 7:** Deprioritize

---

## Messaging by Persona

### CEO / Owner
Lead with business outcome: "Agents who close faster, fewer lost leads, commission you can track."
They care about revenue and control. Don't pitch features — pitch results.

### Head of Sales
Lead with their daily pain: "Do you know right now which leads haven't been followed up in 5 days?"
They manage agents, they feel the chaos. Make them the internal champion.

### GM
Lead with operational clarity: "One place for every lead, every agent, every deal — no more chasing WhatsApp history."
They care about systems. Show them what clean looks like.

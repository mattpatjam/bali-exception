# Lead Sources — Where to Find Jakarta Real Estate Decision-Makers

## Priority Order

1. LinkedIn (richest targeting, best for mid-size agencies)
2. Property portals (Rumah.com, Lamudi, 99.co) — direct agency contacts
3. REI Indonesia (Realestat Indonesia) — member directory
4. Google Maps + website scraping — highest volume, most manual
5. Instagram — for agencies with strong social presence

---

## 1. LinkedIn

**Search strings to use:**
- "CEO" + "real estate" + "Jakarta"
- "Head of Sales" + "properti" + "Jakarta"
- "General Manager" + "agen properti"
- "Direktur" + "properti" + "Jakarta"
- "Owner" + "real estate agency" + "Indonesia"

**Filters:**
- Location: Indonesia → Greater Jakarta
- Industry: Real Estate
- Company size: 11–200 employees (catches our 5–50 agent sweet spot)
- Current role only

**Process:**
1. Search and filter
2. Check company page — confirm they're an agency (not developer/property fund)
3. Check city in their location or company HQ — exclude Bali/Lombok/Sumbawa area
4. Note their name, role, company, and any recent activity (posts, comments) for personalization
5. Send connection request — no message in the request itself
6. After accepted: send opener DM within 48 hours

**Weekly target:** 20 connection requests → expect 5–10 acceptances → 1–2 replies per week

## 2. Property Portals — Rumah.com, Lamudi.co.id, 99.co/id

**How to use:**
- Search for agencies listing properties in Jakarta (Selatan, Pusat, Utara, Barat, Timur)
- On each listing, find the agency name and agent contact
- Visit the agency's website (usually linked from the portal)
- Find contact email or the owner/director name
- Cross-reference on LinkedIn

**What to look for on their website:**
- Does it look manual/outdated? (Pain signal)
- Is there a "team" or "about us" page with a CEO/founder name? (Direct target)
- No chatbot, no CRM-looking workflow? (Confirms they're still analog)

**Volume:** Each portal has 100s of active Jakarta agencies. Expect to identify 50–100 leads from this source in the first month.

**Tool:** Use Firecrawl to scrape agency listings at scale. See `workflows/scrape-property-portals.md` (to be built).

## 3. REI Indonesia (Realestat Indonesia)

**URL:** rei.or.id
**What it is:** The national real estate association. Members include agencies and developers.
**How to use:** Look for the member directory or member list. Cross-reference company names on LinkedIn to find decision-makers.

**Note:** Developers in the REI are not our target — look specifically for agencies (agen, agensi, broker).

---

## 4. Google Maps

**Search queries:**
- "agen properti Jakarta Selatan"
- "agen properti Jakarta Pusat"
- "real estate agency Jakarta"
- "broker properti Jakarta"

**What you get:** Business name, address, website, sometimes phone number.

**Process:**
1. Search and note agencies
2. Visit website to find owner/director name
3. Find them on LinkedIn
4. Add to lead list

**Volume:** High, but quality is mixed. Many small 1–3 agent operations. Filter by checking website size, listing volume, and team size.

---

## 5. Instagram

**How agencies use Instagram in Jakarta:** Post listings, market updates, and team photos. Many Jakarta real estate agencies have 1,000–20,000 followers.

**Search method:**
- Hashtags: #propertijakarta #agenpropertijakarta #rumahjual #jualrumahjkt
- Search "agen properti Jakarta" in accounts

**How to use for outreach:**
- Check if the owner posts personally (they often do on their own account, tagged in agency posts)
- DMs on Instagram are lower friction than email for Indonesian business owners
- Follow first, engage with 1–2 posts, then send a DM 3–5 days later

**Best for:** Agencies that are social-first. Their decision-maker is often personally active on the account.

## Exclusion Check

Before adding any lead, confirm:
- NOT based in or primarily serving Bali
- NOT based in Lombok, Sumbawa, Gili Islands, or nearby islands
- NOT a developer (building/selling own projects) — agency or broker only

If unsure: check their listings on property portals. If they only list in Jakarta and surrounding areas, they're safe to approach.

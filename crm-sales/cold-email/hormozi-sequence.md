# Cold Email — Hormozi-Style Sequence

## Framework Notes

Built on Alex Hormozi's cold outreach approach ($100M Leads):
- Lead with their pain, not your product name
- Give insight or value inside the email — don't just tease it
- One idea per email, under 120 words
- Single low-friction CTA ("worth 20 minutes?" not a calendar link wall)
- Follow-ups must add something new — never just a bump

---

## Opening Messages (Day 0) — 5 Variants

Use one variant per prospect. Match by persona and what you know about their situation.

## Notes

- Never name the product in the first 2 sentences. Pain first, product second.
- Opener 5 is deliberately conversational and slightly unusual — it disarms. Use it when you expect a "not interested" filter otherwise.
- Follow-up 1 is a soft re-pitch with a new hook. Follow-up 2 ends the sequence.
- If they reply with "we already use a CRM" — that's a conversation, not a dead end. Ask which one and what they like/don't like about it.

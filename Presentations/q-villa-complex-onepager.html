Understanding Q's project
Ocean views, non-negotiable. Elevation decides this, more than the address.
Beachfront is a plus, not a requirement. Good to have, not the deciding factor.
One compound, connected underground. Staff and service tunnels link the villas below ground, not just shared grounds.
One family compound, not one villa. A master villa for family, three to four smaller villas for friends.
A full amenity compound. Padel court, spa and gym alongside the villas.
A hybrid model. Personal use two to three months a year, rented out the rest to cover the investment.
Built for decades, not seasons. Poured concrete / ICF-style walls, not brick infill, meant to outlast Bali's standard and pass to his children.
Bringing his own equipment. Construction machinery self-imported from China, drawing on his manufacturing background.
My recommendations

Tabanan

  • Beachfront land available
  • Black sand beaches
  • Better access to water

Bukit

  • Elevated, with ocean views from multiple angles
  • White sand beaches
  • More of a surfer vibe, closer to social life and restaurants
  • Harder, lower-quality water access, being higher up
  • A more hyped-up area
Key challenges:
  • Self-imported machinery (cost and knowledge on how to use). No local spare-parts chain for Chinese-branded equipment.
  • The right workforce to commit to your quality.
  • Underground tunnels and Bali's wet season and humidity.
  • ICF / poured-concrete walls. Bali sits in a seismic zone with inconsistent batching-plant quality.
  • Your price band, tested. At 350–430M/are, ocean-view land in Uluwatu runs closer to 600M/are, Tabanan beachfront closer to 800M–1B/are. Land exists at your number, but you'll need someone with the right contacts to find it.

1. Executive Summary

A Turnkey Asset, Trending Up

Property BE-2386 is a brand-new (built 2025), fully turnkey boutique hotel in Kedungu, one of Tabanan's fastest-growing submarkets, five minutes from Kedungu Beach, with a staffed management team already in place and three diversified revenue lines: rooms, F&B through the on-site Samara Table restaurant, and wellness through Rituals Recovery & Wellness. April 2026, the most recent month of trading on record, was also the strongest since opening. This analysis sets out the trailing financial performance behind that story and translates it into two ready-to-present offer structures for your client.

  • At a 10% required return, the flat cash flow alone almost exactly supports the $1.5M asking price. But 10% is a low required return for an emerging-market, single-asset, leasehold hospitality investment. Institutional buyers typically underwrite Indonesian hospitality at 13% to 18%+.

5.2 Continued

Structuring the Ramp

Because the property is still completing its first full stabilization cycle, we recommend pairing Offer 2 with a short interest-only ramp rather than full amortization from year one (see Section 6 for the numbers behind the recommendation).

Recommended variant, interest-only + balloon: USD 75,000/year interest-only for 5 years, USD 750,000 balloon at year 5. Total nominal consideration USD 1,875,000 (+25% over asking), a larger total, but one that tracks the property's cash flow comfortably from year one and gives the seller an even stronger reason to accept deferred payment.
Blended option: 2 years interest-only (≈ USD 75,000/year) while the property finishes stabilizing, stepping into 3-year amortization of the remaining principal once the higher run-rate is established.

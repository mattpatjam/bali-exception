# Stage 2: Qualification & Discovery

**Objective:** turn a lead into a buyer profile complete enough to shortlist properties for. Do this over WhatsApp, in chat mode, not like a form.

**Previous stage:** [01-lead-generation.md](01-lead-generation.md)
**Next stage:** [03-viewings-property-matching.md](03-viewings-property-matching.md)

---

## Core Philosophy

> "You are not building a sales sequence. You are building a trust sequence that happens to qualify the lead."

WhatsApp is chat mode, not email mode, not a sales page. Short messages, one idea at a time, curiosity over interrogation.

---

## The 5 Principles

1. **First message = short + one question.** The only goal of message #1 is to get message #2. Nothing else.
2. **The question must feel like curiosity, not interrogation.** If it feels like a form, they'll ghost you.
3. **Completeness is a trap.** Don't explain everything upfront. Leave room for them to ask.
4. **Progressive qualification.** Move from easy to sensitive. Never open with budget.
5. **WhatsApp is not email.** Short, casual, one idea per message.

---

## The Intro Message Formula

**Structure:** [context hook] + [one curiosity question]

Bad: "Hi! I'm a real estate agent at Bali Exception Estate and I specialize in villas in Bali and Lombok. I'd love to tell you about our listings. Are you looking to buy?"

Good: "Hey [name] — saw you were asking about Canggu villas. Still looking or you found something?"

Rule: under 2 lines, one question, sounds like a person, not a pitch.

---

## The Follow-Up Sequence

- **Message 2:** react to their answer, ask one more question that goes slightly deeper
- **Message 3:** show you understand their situation, offer one specific insight or option
- **Message 4:** only now move toward logistics (budget framing, timeline, viewing)

Total messages before closing the loop or qualifying: 4-6. Not 1, not 10.

---

## Budget Framing

Never use the word "budget," it implies a ceiling, it sounds like you're figuring out how much to extract.

Instead: **"What are you comfortable investing?"**

This framing implies agency (they choose), removes the ceiling connotation, feels collaborative not transactional. Only ask after trust is established, usually message 4-5 minimum.

---

## What You Need Before Moving to Stage 3

Property matching only works if discovery actually surfaced this. Don't shortlist anything until you have:

- Property type (villa, land, apartment)
- Location preference(s), and how firm vs. flexible they are on that
- Comfortable investment range (not "budget")
- Leasehold vs. freehold preference (or whether they even know the difference yet, if not, that's your next message)
- Purpose: personal residence, rental investment, or both
- Timeline: are they buying now, or exploring for later
- Who else is involved in the decision (spouse, partner, business partner), this matters again at closing, see [04-offer-negotiation-closing.md](04-offer-negotiation-closing.md)

---

## Checklist: Pre-Send Validation

Before sending any WhatsApp message:

- [ ] Is this under 3 lines?
- [ ] Does it contain exactly one question?
- [ ] Does the question feel like curiosity or like a form field?
- [ ] Am I moving the conversation forward or creating friction?
- [ ] Would I send this to a friend? (tone check)

## Checklist: Discovery Complete

Before moving a lead to Stage 3 (property matching):

- [ ] Property type captured
- [ ] Location preference captured, firm vs. flexible noted
- [ ] Comfortable investment range captured (via "comfortable investing," not "budget")
- [ ] Leasehold/freehold preference captured or explained
- [ ] Purpose captured (residence, investment, both)
- [ ] Timeline captured
- [ ] Decision-makers identified (who else needs to be on board)
- [ ] All of the above logged in the CRM, not just left in WhatsApp

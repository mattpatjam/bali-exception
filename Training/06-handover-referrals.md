# Stage 6: Handover & Referral

**Objective:** close out the deal cleanly, and turn a happy buyer into your next lead source. This stage feeds directly back into [01-lead-generation.md](01-lead-generation.md).

**Previous stage:** [05-contracts-legal-process.md](05-contracts-legal-process.md)
**Next stage:** none, this is the end of one deal and the start of the next lead

---

## Handover

- Key handover happens once the property is ready and the final payment has been made
- Every due payment gets a reminder sent to the buyer ahead of time, don't wait for them to chase you or miss a deadline
- Confirm all documentation from Stage 5 (inventory list, floor plan/BOQ if off-plan) is signed and in the buyer's hands, not just the seller's
- Don't consider the deal fully closed until the buyer has confirmed receipt of keys and documents

---

## The Referral Ask

*Source: `.claude/knowledge/hormozi-advisor/knowledge/referrals.md`, applied to Bali Exception in `leads.md`.*

**Timing matters more than technique.** Ask right after the handover, when enthusiasm is highest, not weeks later.

> "You just locked in [property]. Do you know anyone else who's been looking? I'd love an intro, I'll take good care of them."

**Ask WHO, not IF.** Don't say "do you know anyone?" Say "who in your life would benefit from this?" It's a small wording change that gets a real answer instead of a reflexive "I'll think about it."

**Make it easy.** Give them exact language they can forward, don't make them write the pitch themselves. A short WhatsApp message they can copy-paste to a friend works better than "feel free to mention me."

---

## Other Referral Methods Worth Knowing (for later, once volume justifies it)

These aren't required for a new agent's first deals, but worth knowing they exist. Check with Matthieu before formalizing any of these into a standard offer:

- **Two-sided benefit** — split the acquisition cost between the referrer and the new buyer (credit or cash to both). Hormozi's service businesses saw 25-30% of new customers this way
- **Ongoing language** — soft-ask constantly, in every conversation and piece of content, not as a one-time campaign
- **Referral as negotiation chip** — if a buyer asks for a discount, offer instead: "I can't lower the price for you without lowering it for everyone, but if you introduce me to two or three people, I can make this work."

## Checklist: Handover

- [ ] Final payment confirmed received
- [ ] All due-payment reminders were sent on time throughout the process (not just the final one)
- [ ] Inventory list and floor plan/BOQ (if off-plan) signed and in buyer's possession
- [ ] Keys handed over and confirmed received
- [ ] Deal marked closed in the CRM

## Checklist: Referral Ask

- [ ] Ask made within the same conversation as handover, not delayed
- [ ] Asked "who," not "if"
- [ ] Gave the buyer exact language to forward to a friend, not just a vague invitation
- [ ] Any referral that comes in gets logged and routed straight back into [01-lead-generation.md](01-lead-generation.md) as a warm lead

## Shortlisting (4-8 Properties)

- Match against everything captured in discovery: type, location, comfortable investment range, leasehold/freehold, purpose, timeline
- Don't just filter on stated criteria, weigh what they emphasized most in conversation (a buyer who mentioned "quiet" three times cares about that more than a listing that technically fits their price range)
- Include at least one property slightly outside their stated range or area, sometimes the best fit isn't what they asked for. Frame it as an option, not the plan
- Check listing status before adding anything to the shortlist (confirm still available, confirm current asking price, confirm with the lister/seller side if it's been a while since last contact)
- *(Needs Matthieu's input: what does a strong shortlist actually look like in practice? What's the failure mode to avoid — too broad, too narrow, wrong order of presentation?)*

## Organizing the Viewing

- Confirm property availability directly before booking any viewing, not from memory or an old listing
- Sequence viewings logically (geography, not just buyer preference order) to avoid wasted travel time
- Confirm buyer's transport and timing, don't assume
- Brief yourself before each viewing: price, negotiation range if any, key selling points, and anything sensitive about the property (disputes, construction status, access issues) so you're not caught off guard in front of the buyer
- *(Needs Matthieu's input: standard viewing script/talking points per property type, and how to handle a buyer who wants to negotiate on the spot during a viewing)*

## After the Viewing

- Send a recap message same day, not the next day
- Ask for specific feedback: what they liked, what gave them pause, not just "what did you think"
- Log feedback in the CRM against that property, not just in your notes, future shortlists benefit from this
- If interest is real, move immediately to Stage 4. If it's not, go back to discovery, don't just add more properties to the pile, ask what's actually missing

---

## Checklist: Viewing Prep

- [ ] Property availability and asking price reconfirmed before booking
- [ ] Viewing sequence set by geography, not random order
- [ ] Buyer's transport and timing confirmed
- [ ] Briefed on price, negotiation range, and any sensitive property details before arriving
- [ ] Marketing materials or documents ready if the buyer wants to take something away

## Checklist: Post-Viewing Follow-Up

- [ ] Recap sent same day
- [ ] Specific feedback captured (liked / hesitations), not just a vague reaction
- [ ] Feedback logged in CRM against the property
- [ ] Clear next step set: move to offer stage, adjust shortlist, or park the lead with a reason why

## The Full Process, Step by Step

1. Client contact and qualification (Stages 1-2, already done by this point)
2. Villa/property selection and viewings (Stage 3, already done)
3. Offer and negotiation between buyer and seller (Stage 4, already done)
4. Communicate the agreed offer to the team member responsible for the sale
5. The agency's legal team drafts the PSA/LOI, this legally binds buyer and seller
6. Send the LOI to the seller first to check, then to the buyer
7. Buyer chooses the notary for due diligence (agent can recommend a trusted one)
8. Once the pre-sale is signed, the buyer makes the 10% deposit to the notary's escrow account
9. Get proof of deposit from the buyer, send it to the seller and the notary
10. Collect all documentation on the legality of the land and building from the seller side, send to the notary so due diligence can start (takes 2-4 weeks)
11. Notary produces the due diligence report and the draft of the official deed, if DD is validated. If not validated, the buyer's deposit is returned
12. Send the draft to the seller first, then the buyer, to check and agree a signature date
13. Check whether buyer and seller will actually be in Bali for signing, if not, arrange a Power of Attorney (POA) so someone can sign/represent them
14. Buyer, seller, and agent meet at the notary's office to sign the official deed. Payment plan/construction (if applicable) follows from there
15. Set a reminder for each due payment and send it to the buyer
16. Key handover once the property is ready and final payment is made (see [06-handover-referrals.md](06-handover-referrals.md))

## Documents to Request

**From the seller:**
- Copy of passports (buyer & seller)
- PMA company paperwork (if applicable)
- Bank account details
- Floor plan, BOQ, and inventory list
- Original lease contract
- Copy of land certificate
- Copy of landlord's KTP/ID
- Master plan
- Original tax record

**From the buyer:**
- Passports of everyone who will sign
- Confirmation of the notary they've chosen (offer a recommendation if they don't have one)
- Proof of deposit
- Confirmation of whether they'll attend the deed signature in person, if not, request a POA

## Checklist: Contract & Deed Review

There are usually two drafts: one for the land leasehold, one for the building construction. Check both against this list before anything gets signed:

- [ ] ID is correct
- [ ] Client signs under PMA and/or has a Power of Attorney (original PoA sent to the notary)
- [ ] Lease documents and certificate number correct
- [ ] Land size and description of the villa correct
- [ ] Price in IDR correct
- [ ] Payment plan correct
- [ ] Inventory list signed at the deed signature
- [ ] Off-plan: floor plan and BOQ signed at the notary deed signature
- [ ] Bank details of the seller correct
- [ ] Notary fee paid by the buyer, confirmed
- [ ] Force majeure clause present
- [ ] Extension clause present, if applicable
- [ ] Death clause present
- [ ] Bank fee clause present
- [ ] Penalty clause for buyer present
- [ ] Penalty clause for seller present
- [ ] Off-plan: warranty on the building and its time frame stated
- [ ] Access road addressed
- [ ] Government tax responsibility (seller) stated
- [ ] Return-of-object-to-landowner clause present, if applicable
- [ ] Off-plan: PGB/SLF proof-of-process and Pondok Wisata addressed
- [ ] IMB present for finished villas
- [ ] Every article has a property title, price, and date written in letters (not numbers), in black

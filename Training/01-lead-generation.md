# Stage 1: Lead Generation & Prospecting

**Objective:** keep a pipeline of live conversations with people who might buy or invest in Bali/Lombok property. This is the stage that never stops, even mid-deal on other clients.

**Previous stage:** none, this is the start.
**Next stage:** [02-qualification-discovery.md](02-qualification-discovery.md)

---

## The Core Four (in priority order for a new agent)

There are only four ways to generate leads: warm outreach, content, cold outreach, paid ads. Start with warm outreach, it's free and fastest. Add the others as you get comfortable.

### 1. Warm Outreach (start here, day one)

People you already know, past leads who went quiet, and the agency's existing lead database.

**The 9-word probe** (send to any past lead or contact who's gone quiet):
> "Are you still looking for a property in Bali?"

That's it. No pitch. The reply rate on genuinely interested people will surprise you.

**The ACA framework** (Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask) for every reply you get:
1. **Acknowledge** — restate what they said in your own words
2. **Compliment** — tie it to something positive if you can
3. **Ask** — one more question that moves the conversation forward

People love talking about themselves. ACA builds trust fast without ever feeling like a pitch.

**Hidden cost audit** — when a lead goes quiet after a viewing or conversation, it's almost never "the market." Ask:
> "What would have to change for you to feel comfortable moving forward?"

Their answer tells you the real objection. Solve it, or use it to qualify the next person faster.

**Volume benchmark:** 100 warm reach-outs gets you roughly 20 replies, 4 serious conversations, 1 deal. That ratio is the floor, not a target to hit exactly, but if you're doing far fewer than 100 a week you're under-fueling the pipeline.

### 2. Content (build this in parallel, it compounds slowly)

Posting property content, market insight, or behind-the-scenes content on WhatsApp Status, Instagram, or wherever your buyer audience actually is. Not urgent in week one, but the earlier you start, the sooner it compounds. Ask Matthieu which platform to prioritize before investing real time here.

### 3. Cold Outreach (once warm outreach is a habit)

Bali property-buyer interest groups (Facebook, Telegram) are the warmest version of cold, members are already problem-aware, they're looking.

- Reference their specific post or comment before pitching anything, never open cold
- Offer something with real value up front: a free listing-fit consult ("does this property/budget make sense for what you're describing")
- Don't pitch a listing in the first message. Ask a question.

### 4. Paid Ads

Not a new-agent responsibility. Paid ads only work once an offer and message are already proven, this sits with Matthieu/marketing, not with someone still learning the pipeline.

## Referrals as an Ongoing Lead Source

Referrals aren't a one-time ask, see [06-handover-referrals.md](06-handover-referrals.md) for the full referral-ask process at deal close. But keep the door open with existing contacts too: any warm conversation is a chance to ask "do you know anyone else looking?", not just closed clients.

---

## Checklist: Daily / Weekly Lead Generation

Run this every day you're not mid-viewing or mid-close:

- [ ] Sent the 9-word probe to at least 5 past/quiet leads today
- [ ] Followed up on any lead who hasn't replied in 2-3 days (up to 3 attempts before parking them)
- [ ] Logged every new contact and reply in the CRM (not just in WhatsApp)
- [ ] Used ACA on every reply, not just the first message
- [ ] If a lead went quiet after a real conversation, ran the hidden-cost question before writing them off
- [ ] Posted or engaged in at least one relevant buyer interest group this week
- [ ] End of week: counted total outreach attempts and replies, sanity-checked against the 100-reach / 20-reply / 4-conversation benchmark

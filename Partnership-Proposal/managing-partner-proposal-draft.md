# Bali Exception: Managing Partner Proposal (Draft)

*Draft started 2026-08-05, updated 2026-08-06. Working document, not yet sent to Typhaine.*

**Context:** Follows a conversation with Typhaine where Matthieu disclosed he has an outside offer (BSH/Kozystay) and may leave. Typhaine asked him to put together a proposal for taking on a bigger role and helping Bali Exception grow, structured with a salary and commission model similar to what BSH offered. This is that proposal, benchmarked directly against BSH's actual terms.

**Full background:** `Personal/Career/career-decision-2026.md` (Option 6), `Personal/Career/BSH-Kozystay/bsh-terms-recap.md` (the model this is benchmarked against).

---

## 1. The Ask

Matthieu becomes **Managing Partner, Bali Exception.**

This is not a request to take over Typhaine's role. Typhaine stays in day-to-day management: sales leadership, client relationships, deal oversight. What Matthieu brings is the layer underneath that BE doesn't currently have: training systems, process design, org structure, and the business development needed to open new offices. The title reflects a real equity stake, not a claim on Typhaine's current authority.

**Why now:** BE is exposed. FazWaz is expanding aggressively and Bali Home Immo has real digital infrastructure behind it. BE still has no formal training system, new agents learn by shadowing whoever's around, and there's no repeatable process for turning a good salesperson into a second one. 2025 commission revenue was flat against 2024 (-1%), and 2026's pace through July, annualized, points to a real decline (see Section 4). Matthieu has diagnosed exactly what's missing because he's lived inside the gap for five years. The alternative to fixing this now is BE competing on relationships alone against firms that no longer need to.

## 2. Scope of the Role

**Owned by Matthieu:**
- Training and onboarding systems (finishing and launching the agent training framework already drafted in `Bali Exception/Training/`, paused since 2026-07-12)
- SOPs for the full deal lifecycle: lead intake, qualification, viewings, offer/negotiation, contracts, handover
- Org structure and team scalability, building a system that works whether BE has 5 agents or 25
- Digital and marketing systemization, the concrete response to FazWaz and Bali Home Immo's digital reach
- Business development for new entities: Lombok and any future office

**Stays with Typhaine:**
- Day-to-day sales management
- Client relationships and deal oversight
- Business as usual for the existing team

**Reserved for both owners jointly (Typhaine and Raphael):**
- Legal, finance, and admin
- Headcount above an agreed number
- Capital structure of any new entity
- Any decision to sell the company

## 3. Compensation

Benchmarked against BSH's actual offer: IDR 90M/month base (~$67K/year) for a role built from scratch, reporting to an MD, with capped transaction authority. BE is an established, profitable business, not a startup build, and this role carries genuine partner-level scope. That argues for landing above BSH's number.

| Component | Structure |
|---|---|
| **Base salary** | **$85K/year** fixed. Paid regardless of production. This is the floor. |
| **Personal commission** | Standard rate on any deals Matthieu personally closes, once an annual threshold is crossed. Below the threshold, the base salary is treated as covering that production. Threshold to be sized against realistic personal selling bandwidth once the operations workload is clearer, illustrative starting point: 1.5x annualized base salary in personal gross commission. |
| **Profit share** | 15-20% of **incremental EBITDA growth** above the 2025 baseline (~$558K, see Section 4). This is deliberate. Typhaine keeps running day-to-day sales, so Matthieu gets paid for what the restructuring specifically adds, not for production that already existed. |

**Why incremental, not total:** it's the more honest ask. Asking for a cut of BE's existing profit would mean getting paid for Typhaine's ongoing work. Asking for a cut of the growth this role creates ties the number directly to value Matthieu is responsible for.

## 4. Financial Baseline

From BE's own commission recap (all agents combined, 50% to company / 50% to agents):

| Year | Total commission (USD) | Company side (50%) |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | $1,001,262 | $500,631 |
| 2024 | $1,911,792 | $955,896 |
| 2025 | $1,889,792 | $944,896 |
| 2026 (YTD, through July) | $695,949 | $347,975 |

**Overhead (current run-rate, from Matthieu's estimate):** marketing IDR 250M/month + salaries IDR 400M/month + office IDR 50M/month = IDR 700M/month, or roughly **$467K/year** at the ~18,000 IDR/USD rate implied by the commission figures above. Legal department staff costs are already inside the salary line, not a separate cost.

**Legal department revenue:** IDR 120M/month gross, roughly **$80K/year**, on top of commission revenue. Since its costs are already covered by the existing salary overhead, this flows through to EBITDA close to as-is.

**2025 working EBITDA estimate: ~$558K** (company-side commission $944,896, plus $80K legal revenue, minus ~$467K overhead). Used as the baseline year rather than a multi-year average, because today's overhead is a reasonable proxy for late 2025 (8 months prior) but almost certainly overstates costs in 2023-2024, when BE was smaller.

**2026 signal, flagged not asserted:** annualizing the 7-month commission figure ($695,949 → ~$1.19M full-year pace) is roughly a 37% decline in commission revenue versus 2025's pace. Partial-year annualizing ignores seasonality, so this needs a real mid-year gut check with Typhaine before it's used as a hard number, but directionally it supports the urgency case in Section 1.

**Proposed EBITDA growth targets off the 2025 baseline (~$558K), starting point for negotiation:**

| Year | Target | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | Stabilize at baseline (~$558K) | Recover from the 2026 dip before asking for growth on top of it |
| Year 2 | +15% over baseline (~$642K) | First full year of training/org-structure systems in place |
| Year 3 | +30% cumulative (~$726K) | New entity contributing, systems running without Matthieu as bottleneck |
| Year 4 | +50% cumulative (~$837K) | Compounding effect of scale, second new entity or equivalent |

## 5. Equity, earned not granted

No flat equity grant on day one. Equity vests annually against a named KPI set for that year, reviewed jointly with Typhaine (and Raphael) at year-end.

**Target: 20% combined (10% from Typhaine, 10% from Raphael), vesting evenly at 5% per year over 4 years.** Even vesting rather than a back-loaded schedule like BSH's own ESOP (20/45/70/100 cumulative): Matthieu is the one giving up nothing but time and equity risk here, back-loading would mean carrying most of the risk for three years before seeing most of the reward.

| Year | Vests | Cumulative | KPIs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 5% | 5% | Training framework live: Stage 3 (viewings) finished, full lifecycle program running, first agent cohort completed end to end. Deal-lifecycle SOPs documented and used as the default process for every new hire. Org structure v1 proposed and agreed: defined roles/tiers and a promotion path. Active BD support toward the Lombok office opening. EBITDA stabilized at the 2025 baseline (~$558K), recovering from the 2026 dip. |
| Year 2 | 5% | 10% | Training system running for the full existing team, not just new hires (target: ramp-to-first-close time reduced, exact % to agree with Typhaine). Org structure v1 implemented in practice, promotions and team leads named and functioning. Lombok open and producing deals, or a second entity identified and scoped. Digital/marketing systemization live (listing visibility, structured follow-up, paid lead gen, whichever prove out in Year 1). EBITDA +15% over baseline (~$642K). |
| Year 3 | 5% | 15% | Org structure proven at scale: headcount grown without a drop in close rate or deal quality (target headcount % to agree with Typhaine). Second entity opened, or the first entity self-sustaining. Training and SOP systems running without Matthieu as the single point of failure, someone else can run onboarding day to day. EBITDA +30% cumulative over baseline (~$726K). |
| Year 4 | 5% | 20% | Org structure and training system fully embedded as how BE runs, not an ongoing project. A third entity opened or an equivalent expansion milestone. EBITDA +50% cumulative over baseline (~$837K). |

**KPI sign-off:** reviewed jointly by Matthieu and Typhaine (Raphael as needed) at each year-end, with a named dispute/appeal step if there's disagreement on whether a KPI was met. Worth stating explicitly: BSH's own ESOP has no such mechanism, that's a real gap in their deal and this proposal shouldn't repeat it.

**Buyout and liquidity mechanism** (set now, not negotiated later under pressure):
- Pre-agreed valuation formula: a multiple of trailing-12-month EBITDA, refreshed annually
- Put option for Matthieu: force a buyout at the formula price after a minimum hold (not before year 2-3)
- Call option for Typhaine/Raphael: buy him out if he leaves. Good Leaver = full formula price on vested shares. Bad Leaver = discounted, mirroring BSH's own leaver structure
- Tag-along right if BE is ever sold outright

## 6. New Entities (Lombok and beyond)

Kept **separate** from this proposal's equity track. Lombok already has its own negotiation in progress (30% stake, on hold). Any future entity gets its own negotiated stake when it's actually built, rather than folding an unbuilt office into this agreement. What this role contributes to new entities is the business development and setup work, the equity in each new entity is its own conversation.

## 7. What This Solves for Bali Exception

- A real answer to the competitive threat (FazWaz, Bali Home Immo) instead of relying on relationships alone
- A training system that lets BE scale past "everyone learns by shadowing whoever's around"
- A structured digital/marketing response to match Bali Home Immo's reach, not just relationship-based selling
- A tested path to a new office (Lombok, and whatever comes after) without Typhaine or Raphael having to run it personally
- Keeps Matthieu, 5 years of market knowledge and client relationships that would otherwise leave with him if he takes the outside offer
